Home Lab Server & NAS Build

Creating a private and secure storage solution I can reach from anywhere

Motivation

Between my phone, laptop, PC, and both physical and digital media, the only real way to describe my data collection habits would be messy. Trying to find a specific file across all of my devices is like looking for a needle in a digital haystack, especially across platforms, since my phone has a different file system from my PC or laptop.

I thought this was the way things had to be, until I discovered the world of home networking. You could create your own streaming services, host your own games, and even use it as a VPN. Of course, I didn't need all this, but when I learned that there were better solutions than what can only be called a digital dumping ground, there was no turning back.

Hardware Specs

  • Case: 3D-printed testbench-style case
  • Motherboard: ASUS ROG STRIX B550-F
  • CPU: AMD Ryzen 5 3600
  • Storage: 2x Crucial P3 Plus 1TB NVMe SSDs
  • RAM: 2x8GB T-Force Delta DDR4-3200 CL16

Configuration

To ensure maximum uptime and security, I configured the server layer from scratch:

  • Operating System: TrueNAS, chosen for ease of use and headless access.
  • Storage Pooling: RAID 1 across both drives so my data survives a single drive failure.

Conclusion

Setting up a new operating system for the first time proved more difficult than expected. Figuring out the proper dimensions and materials to print was also difficult, but overall I enjoyed the process.
