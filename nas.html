Home Lab Server & NAS Build

Creating a private and secure storage solution I can reach from anywhere

Motivation

Between my phone, my laptop, PC, physical, and digital media, the only real way to describe my data collection habits would be messy. Trying to find a specific file out of all of my devices is like trying to find a needle in a digital haystack. Especially across platforms, where my phone has a different file system from my PC or laptop.

I thought this was the way things had to be, until I discovered the world of home networking. You could create your own streaming services, host your own games, and even use it as a VPN. Of course, I didn't need all this, but when I learned that there were better solutions than what can only be called a digital dumping ground, there was no turning back.

Hardware Specs

  • Case: 3D printed testbench style case
  • Motherboard: Asus ROG STRIX B550-F
  • CPU: Ryzen 5 3600
  • Storage: 2x Crucial P3 Plus 1TB
  • Ram: 2x8GB DDR4 T-Force Delta 3200Mhz CL16

Configuration

To ensure maximum uptime and security, I configured the server layer from scratch:

  • Operating System Used TrueNAS for ease of use and headless access.
  • Storage Pooling: Set up Raid 1 across drives to ensure data safety.

Conclusion

Setting up a new operating system for the first proved more difficult than expected. Figuring out the proper dimensions and materials to print was also difficult, but overall I enjoyed the process.
